GUEST PRIVACY CHARTER

Patron Discretion & Confidentiality Protocol

Discretion is not an afterthought at The Belle Amoura Retreat; it is the cornerstone of our practice. Discerning travellers and high-profile individuals place their privacy in our hands.

Under our strict Confidentiality Standard:

Non-Disclosure Guarantee

Guest identities, travel companions, flight schedules, and villa locations are handled under strict confidentiality and never shared with unauthorized parties.

No Public Marketing of Guests

We do not commercialize, publish, or photograph our patrons for social media, PR, or promotional purposes without express, written permission.

Secure Airside Manifests

All flight manifests for private aviation charters and marine transit are transmitted through encrypted channels solely to authorized civil aviation authorities.

Contractor & Staff Binding

Every Travel Consultant, destination scout, private villa host, and driver is bound by legally enforceable non-disclosure agreements prior to guest deployment.

TRANSPARENT COMMERCE

Curation Terms & Booking Framework

Clear terms protect the integrity of the journey. Every proposal prepared by The Belle Amoura Retreat outlines itemized inclusions, payment milestones, and supplier cancellation policies.

  • Discovery & Consultation: Initial travel consultations are complimentary. Bespoke dossier creation is carried out in considered conversation with the guest.
  • Reservation Confirmation: A booking is formally confirmed once the required deposit is verified and the supplier reservation confirmation is issued in writing.
  • Travel Insurance Requirement: Discerning travellers are required to maintain comprehensive international medical, evacuation, and travel cancellation insurance.
  • Force Majeure & Weather Contingencies: In the event of airspace closures, severe maritime weather, or natural events, our liaison desk enacts immediate alternative itineraries.
SUPPLIER STANDARDS

Supplier Governance & Ethical SLAs

We partner exclusively with verified luxury property owners, private aircraft operators, conservation conservancies, and licensed skippers who uphold our safety and ethical standards.

Safety & Airworthiness

Aviation operators must hold valid Part 135 air carrier certificates (or equivalent) with audited maintenance logs and commercial liability coverage.

Conservation Integrity

Safari lodges must support wildlife conservancies, local indigenous employment, and sustainable solar or low-impact power infrastructure.
