## Composition rules
1. Use one cinematic image per hero, then editorial asymmetry in supporting sections.
2. Keep photography realistic, immersive and location-specific. Avoid generic AI art and repeated card compositions.
3. Prefer edge-to-edge photography, square-edged editorial cards, and carefully spaced typography.
4. Use pearl text on obsidian or deep peacock; use peacock teal for calls to action and interactive states.
5. Use brass sparingly for thin dividers, focus states and small details.
6. Use Playfair Display for expressive headings and Lora for reading, forms and long-form articles.
7. Mobile must retain image impact and never create horizontal overflow or blank spacer sections.
8. Keep logo art as a real image asset. Do not ask image generators to redraw or approximate the official logo.
