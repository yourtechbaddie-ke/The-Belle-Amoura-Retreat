Privacy, with care.

When you submit a travel enquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, destination interests and message, so the team can respond to your request. Please do not submit passport numbers, payment-card details or sensitive identity documents through the enquiry form.

How information is used

Enquiry details are used to review and respond to your travel request and to help with follow-up about that request. They should not be treated as a confirmed booking or payment instruction. If a third-party supplier is involved, that supplier may separately collect information under its own privacy notice and terms.
